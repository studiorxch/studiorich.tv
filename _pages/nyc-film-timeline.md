<h2>New York City on Film</h2>

<p>
A living timeline of movies that captured the streets, neighborhoods, and mythology of New York City.
</p>

<p>
From the silent-era Manhattan of <em>The Crowd</em> (1928) to modern films like <em>Uncut Gems</em> and <em>Joker</em>, these works map nearly a century of the city's visual culture — crime films, underground cinema, graffiti documentaries, romantic comedies, and independent street portraits.
</p>

<p>
Each entry reflects a film where <strong>New York is not just a backdrop, but a character</strong> — shaping the story, its people, and the atmosphere of the streets.
</p>
